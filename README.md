## What is Rosuii?

Rosuii is a bilingual Bangla–English restaurant management platform built for restaurants in Bangladesh. It brings the front counter, kitchen and back office into one browser-based workspace.

রসুই বাংলাদেশের রেস্টুরেন্ট, ক্যাফে, ক্লাউড কিচেন ও মাল্টি-ব্রাঞ্চ ব্যবসার জন্য তৈরি একটি বাংলা–ইংরেজি restaurant management software। POS, অনলাইন অর্ডার, মেনু, স্টক, কিচেন, স্টাফ, ডেলিভারি ও রিপোর্ট এক জায়গা থেকে পরিচালনা করা যায়।

## Core capabilities

- POS for dine-in, takeaway and delivery
- Branded online ordering and table QR flow from Starter
- Menus, variations, add-ons and combos
- Kitchen Display System and Customer Display System from Growth
- Inventory, suppliers, purchasing, production and wastage from Starter
- Tables, floors and reservations
- Attendance, roles, payroll and salary advances
- Customer profiles, coupons and loyalty
- Rider assignment and delivery-status tracking
- Sales, expenses, profit-and-loss and day-close reports
- Multi-branch management with an isolated database per restaurant
- Bangla and English interface across compatible browser-based devices

Feature access varies by plan. Verify the current catalogue at [rosuii.com/features](https://rosuii.com/features).

## Important product boundaries

- Online ordering starts on Starter; it is not part of Free.
- Eligible POS orders can queue during an internet outage and sync afterward. Online-payment confirmation and real-time KDS updates still need connectivity.
- Eligible Pro restaurants can configure their own bKash or Nagad gateway credentials.
- Card can be recorded as a payment entry; Rosuii does not currently claim a production card-gateway integration.
- This repository does not publish unsupported customer counts, ratings, uptime or ROI claims.
