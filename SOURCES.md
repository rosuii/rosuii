## Maintenance rules

1. Recheck pricing and plan limits against the canonical pricing page before every public update.
2. Update the visible “last verified” date, JSON-LD `dateModified`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt` and sitemap together.
3. Keep limitations beside related feature claims.
4. Never add customer counts, ratings, uptime, revenue, funding, ROI or performance statistics without a linkable source and company approval.
5. Never expose real tenant, customer, order, staff or financial information in screenshots.
6. Add new directory and social URLs to the official site's Organization `sameAs` only after the profiles are live and controlled by Rosuii.
