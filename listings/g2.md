## Full product description

> Rosuii is a browser-based restaurant management platform built for food businesses in Bangladesh. Its bilingual Bangla–English workspace brings together dine-in, takeaway and delivery POS, branded direct online ordering, table QR menus, menu management, kitchen and customer displays, inventory, purchasing, production, wastage, reservations, expenses, attendance, payroll, CRM, loyalty, rider management and business reports.
>
> Each restaurant receives a branded subdomain and an isolated database. Rosuii can queue eligible POS orders on the device during an internet outage and synchronize them when connectivity returns; real-time KDS updates and online-payment confirmation still require internet access.
>
> Plans begin with Free for one branch and up to 20 daily orders. Online ordering starts on Starter. KDS and CDS also start on Starter; payroll, loyalty and rider management start on Growth. Eligible Pro restaurants can configure their own bKash or Nagad gateway credentials. Current plan access and limits should be verified on the official pricing page.

## Ideal customer profile

> Independent restaurants, cafés, quick-service restaurants, cloud kitchens and multi-branch restaurant operators in Bangladesh that need a bilingual system for front-of-house, kitchen and back-office work.

## Problems Rosuii addresses

- Disconnected order channels and manual ticket handling
- Paper or spreadsheet-based stock and purchasing records
- Limited visibility into expenses, sales and profit
- Separate systems for kitchen, staff, delivery and customer records
- Software that does not fit Bangla-language restaurant teams
- Hardware lock-in and difficulty managing more than one branch

## Core capabilities

- POS for dine-in, takeaway and delivery
- Menu items, modifiers, add-ons and combos
- Table, floor and reservation management
- Branded online ordering and table QR flow
- Kitchen Display System and Customer Display System
- Inventory, suppliers, purchase orders, production and wastage
- Attendance, roles, payroll and salary advances
- CRM, coupons and loyalty
- Rider assignment and delivery-status workflow
- Sales, item, expense, profit-and-loss and day-close reports
- Multi-branch controls and isolated restaurant databases
- Bangla and English interface

## Three differentiators

1. **Built for Bangladesh:** Bangla–English workflows, BDT pricing and eligible bKash/Nagad gateway configuration.
2. **One restaurant workspace:** Front counter, direct ordering, kitchen, stock, staff, customers, delivery and reports share the same system.
3. **Data separation and flexible access:** Each restaurant receives an isolated database and branded subdomain, with access from compatible browser-based devices.
