## Full company description

> Rosuii develops browser-based restaurant management software for food businesses in Bangladesh. Its bilingual Bangla–English platform combines point of sale for dine-in, takeaway and delivery, branded online ordering, table QR menus, menu management, kitchen and customer displays, inventory, purchasing, staff attendance, payroll, customer management, loyalty, rider delivery and business reporting.
>
> The product serves independent restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens and growing multi-branch operators. Each restaurant is provisioned with a branded subdomain and an isolated database. Rosuii publishes Free, Starter, Growth and Pro plans in Bangladeshi Taka, with feature access and usage limits varying by plan.
