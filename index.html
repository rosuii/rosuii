Verified feature overview

What restaurants can manage with Rosuii

Restaurant POS

Handle dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders from one screen, with tables, waiters, modifiers, discounts, service charge, VAT and receipt printing.

Offline order queue

Eligible POS orders can be queued on the device during an outage and synced after connectivity returns. Online payments and real-time KDS updates still require internet.

Direct online ordering

From Starter, publish a branded restaurant storefront with menu, cart, checkout, order tracking and table-QR ordering on a Rosuii subdomain.

Menu management

Manage categories, items, bilingual names, photos, sizes, variations, add-ons, combos, preparation times and kitchen stations.

KDS and CDS

Growth includes kitchen and customer displays for preparing orders, tracking order age and showing ready token numbers.

Inventory and purchasing

Track stock items, units, costs, suppliers, purchase orders, production, wastage and low-stock thresholds from Starter.

Tables and reservations

Organize floors and tables, generate per-table QR access and receive reservation requests. Reservation access begins on Starter.

Staff and payroll

Maintain employee records, roles, branch access, attendance, shifts, salary advances and payroll. Full payroll begins on Growth.

Customers and loyalty

Keep customer profiles and order history, then manage loyalty points and coupons where the selected plan permits.

Delivery workflow

Assign riders and follow delivery status from pickup to completion. Rider management is included from Growth.

Reports and finance

Review sales, items, staff, expenses, profit and loss, day-close reports and exports according to plan availability.

Multi-branch isolation

Each restaurant receives its own isolated database and branded subdomain. Branch limits range from one on Free to unlimited on Pro.

Bangladesh-ready payments

Record cash, card and cash-on-delivery. Eligible Pro restaurants can configure their own bKash or Nagad gateway credentials.

Works across devices

Use Rosuii from compatible phones, tablets, laptops and browser-based POS terminals without being locked to one hardware vendor.

Roles and permissions

Give owners, managers, cashiers, waiters and kitchen staff access to the tools appropriate for their work.

Accuracy note: Feature availability and usage limits vary by plan. Always verify changing details on the official feature page and official pricing page.

Frequently asked questions

Quick, citable answers

What is Rosuii?

Rosuii is a bilingual, browser-based restaurant management platform built for restaurants in Bangladesh. It combines POS, direct online ordering, menus, inventory, kitchen operations, staff, payroll, CRM, delivery and reports, with availability varying by plan.

Does Rosuii have a free plan?

Yes. Free is an ongoing ৳0 plan with one branch and up to 30 orders per day. It includes POS, menu, tables, customers and basic reports. Online ordering starts on Starter.

Can Rosuii bill during an internet outage?

Rosuii can queue eligible POS orders on the device during an outage and sync them after connectivity returns. It does not mean every function is fully offline: online-payment confirmation and real-time KDS updates require a connection.

Does Rosuii support Bangla?

Yes. Rosuii supports Bangla and English across its restaurant workflows.
